\section{Study 2: Five Historical Strategies Across a Broad Universe}
\label{sec:study2design}

\subsection{Pre-registration and single source of truth}

Every Study 2 design choice --- universe, cleaning rules, strategies and parameter grids, components,
freeze dates, execution model, statistics, regime definitions, and crisis windows --- is declared in
one configuration file (\texttt{study.toml}) that was committed to version control \emph{before} any
Study 2 data was downloaded or any result existed. The implementation reads nothing else: the same
file drives data collection, every experiment, and every table, figure, and in-text number of this
section and the next, which are generated by the pipeline rather than typed
(Appendix~\ref{sec:repro}). The only deviation from the pre-registration was forced by data
availability and is logged: one planned symbol (Roche, \texttt{ROG.SW}) was not served by the data
source, leaving \STwoInstruments{} of \STwoPlanned{} planned series. The regime-filter component was
restricted in advance to a single label definition so that it could not be chosen after the fact.

\subsection{Data and provenance}

Daily OHLC data for \STwoInstruments{} instruments in \STwoMarkets{} markets (Table~\ref{tab:s2data})
--- \STwoNequityindices{} equity indices from the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific,
\STwoNequityetfs{} US sector and country ETFs, \STwoNfixedincome{} bond ETFs and Treasury futures,
\STwoNcommodities{} commodity futures, \STwoNfx{} currency pairs including emerging-market currencies,
\STwoNcrypto{} cryptocurrencies, and \STwoNstocksus{}~US plus \STwoNstocksintl{} non-US large-cap
stocks on \STwoExchanges{} exchanges --- were downloaded from the Yahoo Finance chart API
(fetched \STwoFetched{}, \STwoFirst{} to \STwoLast{}, \STwoRows{} daily bars after cleaning). The raw
API responses are stored byte-for-byte with their URL, UTC fetch time, and SHA-256 hash; cleaning is
a deterministic function of the raw bytes and the declared rules (split/dividend adjustment via the
adjusted close; removal of \STwoDropped{} rows with missing or non-positive prices; \STwoRepaired{}
OHLC-consistency repairs; \STwoBadTicks{} reversing bad ticks). Two independent checks validate the
data: against \STwoFredN{} FRED series (5-day return correlation \STwoFredCorrFiveMin{}--\STwoFredCorrFiveMax{},
median absolute price difference at most \STwoFredMedDiffMax{}; daily FX correlations are lower only
because Yahoo and FRED fix FX at different times of day, and natural-gas front-month futures vs.\ Henry
Hub spot, a different instrument, correlate at \STwoNgCorrFive{}), and a browser spot-check of
\STwoSpotDays{} closing prices for \STwoSpotSymbols{} symbols against MarketWatch (maximum
difference \STwoSpotMaxDiff{}).

\subsection{The five strategies}

Five historically documented rule families serve as controls (Table~\ref{tab:s2strat}): the JMA
crossover of Study 1; the dual moving-average crossover of \citet{brock1992simple}; the Donchian
channel breakout of the Turtle rules \citep{faith2007turtle}; time-series momentum
\citep{moskowitz2012momentum}, entered when the sign of the trailing 6- or 12-month return flips; and
RSI(2) short-term mean reversion in the direction of the 200-day trend \citep{connors2008short}. Four
are trend-following, one is contrarian, and their holding periods range from days to months. Each
control's parameters are walk-forward selected per instrument exactly as in Study 1.

\subsection{Execution and portfolio accounting}

Execution is deliberately conservative. Signals and exits are evaluated on daily closes; a stop
breached by a close is filled \emph{at that close}, not at the stop level (the optimistic stop-level
fill of Study 1 is re-run as a sensitivity), and entries fill at the signal bar's close (a one-day
entry lag is re-run as a sensitivity). Round-trip transaction cost plus slippage is set per market in
advance (\STwoCostfx{}\,bps FX, \STwoCostequityindices{}\,bps indices/ETFs/futures, \STwoCoststocksus{}\,bps single stocks, \STwoCostcrypto{}\,bps crypto) and varied
\STwoCostMults{}. Performance is measured on \emph{mark-to-market} daily portfolios:
capital is split equally across the instruments of a market (or of the whole universe) that trade on a
given day, each position earns its instrument's close-to-close return from entry to exit, and half
the round-trip cost is charged at entry and half at exit, scaled by position size. We report CAGR,
volatility, Sharpe and Sortino ratios (no risk-free rate, since the universe spans currencies),
maximum drawdown, daily 95\% CVaR, skewness, one-way turnover, and trade statistics.

\subsection{Windows, generalization, and robustness design}

Two independent freezes give two non-overlapping out-of-sample windows, each with its own frozen
models: \textbf{F\STwoFzA{}} (models trained on \STwoStart--\STwoFzATrainEnd, tested \STwoFzA--\STwoFzAEnd, including the global
financial crisis) and \textbf{F\STwoFzB{}} (trained on \STwoStart--\STwoFzBTrainEnd, tested \STwoFzB{} onwards, including the COVID
crash and the 2022 bear market). Crypto enters only F2018. Each (strategy, market, freeze) comparison
is a market-level test; the equal-weight portfolio of all \STwoInstruments{} instruments gives a
global test per (strategy, freeze). Beyond the main grid, the design pre-registers: \emph{cross-market
transfer} (meta-labeling and meta-label + sizing models fit on every \emph{other} market, then applied
to the held-out market); \emph{budget ablations} (meta-label thresholds \STwoThrLo{} and \STwoThrHi{}; a small model
with \STwoSmallTrees{} trees of depth \STwoSmallDepth{} for meta-labeling and exit); \emph{execution} sensitivities; cost multipliers;
\emph{cross-regime} tests (bull/bear by the market index's position vs.\ its 200-day average, high/low
volatility by 20-day realized volatility vs.\ its expanding median, both known at $t-1$); five crisis
windows; a survivorship split (survivorship-free indices/ETFs/futures/FX vs.\ current-constituent
stocks and surviving coins); leave-one-instrument-out and leave-one-market-out jackknives; and an
in-sample vs.\ out-of-sample decay check of the controls' parameter selection.

\subsection{Inference}

The difference in annualized Sharpe ratio between a variant and its control is tested with a paired
circular-block bootstrap \citep{politis1992circular,ledoit2008robust}: both daily return series are
aligned on the same dates and the \emph{same} \STwoBlock-day blocks are resampled for both (\STwoBoot{}
replicates), preserving their cross-correlation and short-range serial dependence --- correcting the
unpaired i.i.d.\ test of Study 1. The two-sided $p$-value is $2\min\{P(\Delta^{*}\le0),P(\Delta^{*}\ge0)\}$.
Multiplicity is controlled with the Benjamini--Hochberg false-discovery rate \citep{benjamini1995fdr}
within pre-defined families (the \STwoMktTests{} market-level main tests; the \STwoGlobTests{} global
main tests; transfer; ablation; execution), with Holm's method \citep{holm1979simple} reported as a
family-wise check, in the spirit of \citet{harvey2016cross} and \citet{sullivan1999data}. A difference
is called significant iff $q<\STwoAlpha$. Deflated Sharpe ratios \citep{bailey2014deflated} are reported for
every portfolio.
